    FAQ

    Questions Before You Start

    Clear answers about coaching, what is included, and how the system works.
    WHAT HAPPENS AFTER I PAY?
    You create your account, finish a short assessment with progress photos, and get a personal workout + diet within 24–48 hours. Then you use daily trackers, message your coach, submit weekly check-ins, and receive plan updates based on your real progress.
    WHAT EXACTLY IS INCLUDED?
    Personal workout, personal diet, weekly coach check-ins, daily trackers, coach chat, progress photos/journey, Consistency League, and weekly plan updates. One price — no separate upsell for core coaching.
    WILL I GET A REAL COACH?
    Yes. A human coach reviews your assessment, check-ins, and progress. Tools help them work faster — they do not replace the coach. You also get direct coach chat inside the app.
    GYM OR HOME? BEGINNERS? VEGETARIANS?
    All welcome. Plans are built for gym, home, or mixed setups, matched to your experience level, and diet is customized for vegetarian, vegan, and allergy needs.
    WHAT IS THE CONSISTENCY LEAGUE?
    A monthly accountability ladder inside the LURVOX app. Entry is free with every coaching plan. You earn points from daily trackers, weekly check-ins, progress photos, and streaks. Top 10% in your division promote each month: Bronze → Silver → Gold → Platinum → Diamond (certificates & trophies). Crazy 1/2/3 is the prize-money arena — up to ₹5,000 — and requires the 12-month plan. World Leaderboard coming soon. Open /pages/consistency-league for the full breakdown.
    WHAT IF I MISS WORKOUTS OR WANT TO CANCEL?
    Tell your coach on the next check-in — plans adjust for busy weeks, travel, and missed sessions. Consistency over perfection. To cancel, message us on WhatsApp and we will process it without pressure games.
