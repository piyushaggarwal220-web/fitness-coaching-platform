    FAQ

    Questions before you start

    Straight answers on coaching, what is included, and how the system works — before you pick a plan.

    What happens after I pay?

    You create your account, finish a short assessment with progress photos, and get a personal workout + diet within 24 to 48 hours. Pay securely with UPI / cards on Razorpay. Then use daily trackers, message your coach, submit weekly check ins, and get plan updates from real progress.

    What exactly is included?

    Personal workout, personal diet, weekly coach check ins, daily trackers, coach chat, progress photos/journey, and weekly plan updates. One price. No separate upsell for core coaching.

    Will I get a real coach?

    Yes. A human coach reviews your assessment, check ins, and progress. Tools help them work faster. They do not replace the coach. You also get direct coach chat inside the app.

    Gym or home? Beginners? Vegetarians?

    All welcome. Plans are built for gym, home, or mixed setups, matched to your experience level, and diet is customized for vegetarian, vegan, and allergy needs.

    Does my coach call me?

    On the 12 month plan, your coach calls you every week. Chat and check ins are on every plan; the weekly phone call is not included on 3 or 6 months.

    What if I want a refund?

    Cancellation anytime on WhatsApp. No pressure games. Full refund if onboarding is complete and we don’t deliver your first plan within 48 hours (request within 7 days of purchase). After plan delivery / coaching use, purchases are generally non refundable. Separately, our results guarantee applies if you submit at least 90% of due check ins on time and still report no result (admin reviewed). Details: refund policy and app.lurvox.in/refund-policy.
