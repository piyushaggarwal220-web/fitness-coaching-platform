## Client plan invariants

1. Weekly diet calories follow the training workload wave. Raise calories modestly as volume and intensity rise through a four week cycle. At a new split with lower base volume, reduce calories slightly from the prior peak and repeat the rise.
2. Client visible plan content must contain no hyphen or dash characters. Write numeric ranges with the word `to`, write compound words with spaces, and use unprefixed lines for lists.
